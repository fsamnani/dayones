The new formula

Heat = 15 + 10 × log2(this week ÷ own 8-week average), clamped to 0–50

Half their normal attention scores 5, a normal week 15, double 25, eight times 45. The 15 is a tuning knob (heatBaselinePoints). The order of who scored more than whom is unchanged.

Two side effects to decide on

Talent with no countable signal now fall far behind. Under the old formula a missing signal and a normal week both scored 0. Now a normal week is 15 and a missing signal is still 0. In the live league this week, 51 of 100 draftable talent score 0 Heat: those without a Wikipedia page, until YouTube and Twitch Heat switch on around Dec 14, plus small pages under the noise floor. They are mostly the small talent the game wants people to draft.

The Day One multiplier now multiplies the 15. A 3x pick earns about 3 × 13 ≈ 39 Heat in an ordinary week just for being on the roster, against 13 for a 1x pick. Over a 12-week season that is roughly 491 versus 164, before anyone has grown or spiked. Under the old formula the multiplier only multiplied real movement.

Sunday-night timing

Wikipedia reports days in UTC and has no hourly numbers for a single page, so the approved hourly fix is not possible. The alternative is to move each Wikipedia day back by one before assigning it to a week. Sunday evening in the US is Monday in UTC, so this puts Sunday-night events in the right week, at the cost of moving Monday daytime views into the previous week.

Decisions for Faraz

  1. Talent with no countable Heat signal: (a) leave at 0, since most gain YouTube or Twitch Heat by Dec 14, before anyone plays; or (b) give them the normal-week 15 until a signal exists, so small talent are not penalized for missing data.
  2. Multiplier and the 15: (a) multiply everything, as now; or (b) leave the 15 unmultiplied and multiply only Growth, Heat above 15, and milestones, so the multiplier rewards small talent who move, not small talent who sit still.
  3. Sunday-night timing: hourly is not available. Approve the one-day shift instead?
  4. Shows with several hosts: track every host's Wikipedia page (Akaash Singh for Flagrant, Jack Selby for Iced Coffee Hour, Francis Foster for TRIGGERnometry) with the strongest one winning?
