# Scoring review log

The scoring system exists to measure how much public attention a talent commands. If points disagree with what is obviously happening in the world, the formula is wrong, not reality.

Add an entry every week the score missed something obvious. When a formula change follows, link the commit and announce it to the league.

## How to add an entry

Copy this block to the top of the Entries section and fill it in.

```
### Week of YYYY-MM-DD: short title
- **Talent:** who
- **What happened in the world:** the obvious thing (with a link)
- **What the score said:** points that week, and why that felt wrong
- **Likely cause:** missing signal, bad weight, cap too low, data gap, etc.
- **Action:** formula change, new data source, or "watch for now"
- **Status:** open / fixed in <commit> / won't fix
```
